## 2. Expert-vs-expert spread: the widest disagreements

The big ones run **30–50+ overall picks** between analysts on the same player, and they cluster on three causes: **injury-recovery timelines, coaching/scheme changes, and role ambiguity.**

- **Malik Nabers (NYG WR)** — range ~22–49 overall (ADP 29). Double ACL recovery; Pianowski fades entirely, others hold top-30 ([Yahoo's most-debated players](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/2026-fantasy-football-from-trey-mcbride-to-malik-nabers-here-are-the-players-our-yahoo-analysts-have-debated-the-most-in-their-rankings-125353051.html)).
- **George Kittle** — ~50-pick split. January Achilles at age ~33.
- **Travis Etienne** — 33rd overall to Round 5 (~40+ spots). Backfield/OL ambiguity.
- **Trey McBride** — TE1 at ECR ~29 but some analysts dock a full round on the new Arizona play-caller.
- **Bucky Irving** — RB19 consensus; individual calls span RB19→RB38 (offseason injury vs Zac-Robinson-upside).
- **Josh Jacobs** — 27th to 40+ (suspension uncertainty + age).
- **Kyler Murray** — top-5 QB finish to complete avoidance (job security vs McCarthy).
- **Rashee Rice**, **Jaylen Waddle**, **Oronde Gadsden II**, **Parker Washington**, **Jordan Love** — smaller but notable analyst splits (role structure, target-volume assumptions).

The std-dev column on FantasyPros pages is the systematic version of this list — high-σ players are exactly where "consensus" is a label, not an agreement.

## 3. Format-driven differences

**PPR vs Standard** ([SI projections comparison](https://www.si.com/onsi/fantasy/rankings/fantasy-football-ppr-formats-scoring-projections-each-position-2026)):
- Risers in PPR: Garrett Wilson +9 (biggest WR mover, ~4.9 rec/gm), De'Von Achane +6, Chris Olave +4, Rashee Rice / DJ Moore +3; TEs Jake Ferguson +5, Hockenson +4, Schultz +3.
- Fallers in PPR: Alec Pierce −8 (boom-bust, low catch volume), McLaurin / Odunze / Watson −3; big-play TEs Kraft, Kittle, Gadsden −3 to −4.
- Half-PPR moves are roughly half these magnitudes; QB/K/DST barely move.

**1QB vs Superflex:** the largest format effect in fantasy. QB20 outscores a typical RB2/WR2 most weeks, so all 32 starters + backups get drafted and experts advise rostering 3–4 QBs ([FantasyPros superflex strategy](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/08/superflex-fantasy-football-draft-strategy-qb2-targets-2026/)). This is a scarcity-denominator change, not a value-model change — exactly how the Sidekick PRD treats format (§7).

## 4. Platform-vs-platform differences

- **TreVeyon Henderson (RB): 23-spot ADP spread** — ESPN ~28.8 vs Yahoo/CBS ~41.8 vs Sleeper 51.8 ([FantasyLife platform comparison](https://www.fantasylife.com/articles/fantasy/must-draft-players-on-espn-sleeper-yahoo-fantasy-football-2026/)).
- **DeVonta Smith:** late-4th on Sleeper vs 2/3 turn in best ball — the sharp market prices him ~2 rounds earlier.
- Structural finding: **ESPN and Sleeper default ranks lag** ("badly outdated or otherwise questionable"), Yahoo skews sharper; best-ball/NFFC markets lead, casual-platform ADP follows. Rooms drafting off platform defaults are predictably behind the expert consensus — an exploitable, per-platform bias.
- **ESPN vs FantasyPros philosophically:** ESPN surfaces ~8 named staff rankers individually; FantasyPros smooths 153 into one number. Consensus hides the variance ESPN exposes.

## 5. Criticisms of consensus (why a plain ECR mirror isn't enough)

- **Herding / slow adaptation:** ECR mutes sharp contrarian calls and reacts slowly to role changes — many individual lists must move before the aggregate does ([FantasyPros' own value-picks discussion](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/07/fantasy-football-value-picks-by-ecr-2026/)). Example: Jonathan Brooks at ECR 110 vs 91 at a single outlet that has conviction on the knee.
- **Rankings vs projections diverge on the same site:** Caleb Williams ECR QB7 vs projection QB13; Jaxson Dart ECR QB11 vs projection QB6. Expert contextual judgment and pure models disagree by 3–6 QB slots routinely ([rankings-vs-projections](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/07/fantasy-football-rankings-vs-projections-quarterbacks-2026/)).
- **Consensus is robust, not optimal:** FantasyPros' own accuracy contest shows repeat individual winners (Boone 2019+2025, Thorman four straight top-4) — persistent skill exists above the consensus ([accuracy methodology](https://www.fantasypros.com/about/faq/football-draft-accuracy-methodology/)).
- **Stale lists drag the average** — mitigated by FantasyPros' recency weighting, but per-expert update cadence is uncontrolled.

## 6. Implications for willy-ff

1. **Super-consensus:** keep variance (σ, min/max) and per-source staleness as first-class columns; weight by demonstrated accuracy and recency rather than mirroring ECR. The gap categories above (injury-timeline, coaching-change, role-ambiguity) deserve explicit uncertainty flags.
2. **Draft Sidekick:** platform matters twice — the room's ADP prior should be **platform-specific** (a Sleeper room prices Henderson ~2 rounds later than an ESPN room; open question 2 in the PRD is all but answered), and platform default-rank lag is a predictable bias for the behavioral model.
3. **High-σ players are where availability projections must widen** — feeding PRD v3 §3.5's disagreement signal.
