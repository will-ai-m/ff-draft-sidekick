Draft-day takeaways

  • Chargers (D#6) have the best early combo of unit + schedule: ARI and LV out of the gate, four soft-or-neutral weeks of five.
  • Jaguars (D#9) open with the league's worst projected offense (CLE, 40.5 total, −7) — a week-1 smash spot.
  • Broncos (D#1) are the best unit but draw a brutal slate: @KC, LAR, @SF, @LAC in the first five. Talent vs. schedule tradeoff.
  • 49ers (D#18) and Patriots (D#8) are prime streamer targets — SF gets MIA + ARI in weeks 2–3, NE gets LV in week 5.
  • Seahawks (D#3) pair an elite unit with @ARI in week 2; Vikings (D#7) close weeks 4–5 with MIA and @NO.
  • Fade early: Bills and Rams defenses face three top-10 offenses each. KC and CAR are on bye in week 5.
