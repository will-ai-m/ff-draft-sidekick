## 2. Coordinator details (the ones that matter most)

### Offensive coordinators — high leverage

- **BAL — Declan Doyle** (Bears OC 2025, didn't call plays; Payton's Saints/Broncos → Ben Johnson). Youngest play-caller in NFL. Minter: "architect of offense." Expect heavier motion/shifts, gap+zone mix, formation variance (inference from tree). Backstop: Joe Lombardi (senior asst), Marcus Brady (PGC). OL/TE turnover: Linderbaum → LV (3yr/$81M), Likely → NYG (FA, 3yr/$40M); drafted G Vega Ioane #14. Flowers extended 4yr/$140M; Andrews extended 3yr/$39.3M.
- **ATL — Tommy Rees** (Browns OC; called plays from Wk 10 '25 — confirmed). His rookies produced on a bad team: Fannin 72-731-6, Judkins 827 yds. Running Stefanski's system. Staff: Tanner Engstrand PGC (ex-Jets OC/Lions PGC), Bill Callahan OL, Van Pelt QBs.
- **LAC — Mike McDaniel** (fired Dolphins HC, 35-33). Miami hallmarks confirmed: zone runs 54–60%, motion 67.5% (league-high). Camp reports (single-source): outside-zone-heavy, quick-timing, 12/13 personnel, faster tempo. Signed David Njoku (TE2 behind Oronde Gadsden II); Keenan Allen departed.
- **DET — Drew Petzing** (fired Cardinals OC; 9 yrs under Stefanski). ARI 2025: **67.1% neutral pass rate, NFL-highest** (PFF-confirmed) but 24th in EPA/play; elite TE usage (**McBride 169 targets**, TE record); downhill RB workloads (Conner). Montgomery traded to HOU; **Isiah Pacheco signed** as replacement.
- **TB — Zac Robinson** (Falcons OC 2024–25). Confirmed: play-action rose 13.8% (2024, last) → 22% (2025); ~37% pistol (2024); Bijan 287 carries + ~100 targets; London 112 targets in 12 games; Pitts 928 yds. Criticisms: predictable, 31st on 3rd down. Neutral-pass-rate/motion ranks ⚠️(2024 figures). Evans → SF; Gainwell signed.
- **NYJ — Frank Reich** (Colts/Panthers HC, Eagles/Chargers OC). Adapts to QB; RPO/rhythm passing; historically RB-heavy. Last 500-yd TE under him: Ebron 2018 (confirmed). "Only 3 WRs hit 15 PPG in 10 seasons" ⚠️(unsourced). Breece Hall re-signed 3yr/$45.75M.
- **NYG — Matt Nagy** (Chiefs OC 2023–25). Reid tree; only "half installed" by spring, building around Dart. Greg Roman (senior asst) and Brian Callahan (PGC/QBs) also in the room — "insurance" framing is speculation.
- **TEN — Brian Daboll** (fired Giants HC; built Josh Allen). Ward preseason struggles reported by Saleh ("off-script playmaker to station-to-station"). Wan'Dale Robinson signed as starting slot; Carnell Tate R1.
- **WAS — David Blough** (asst QB coach, first-time caller). Quinn: "more under center," more play-action. Kingsbury → Rams asst HC. Diggs signed (1yr, up to $12M). **LT Laremy Tunsil tore triceps.**
- **MIA — Bobby Slowik** (ex-Texans OC). "Skew 50-50 on early downs," Shanahan-tree. Kevin Patullo (fired PHI OC) is PGC. WR room gutted (Hill cut, Waddle → DEN by trade).
- **PHI — Sean Mannion** (Packers QB coach 2025; offensive asst 2024; never called a play). Shanahan-tree via McVay/Kubiaks/LaFleur: more under center, wide zone, boot, motion. Grizzard (fired TB OC) is PGC. A.J. Brown traded to NE (June 1); R1 WR Makai Lemon.
- **SEA — Brian Fleury** (49ers TE coach 2022–25 + run-game coord 2025). Stated goal: "maintain as much [of Kubiak's offense] as possible." Kenneth Walker → KC (FA, 3yr/$43.05M); Charbonnet torn ACL (Jan) ⚠️Week 1; drafted Jadarian Price R1.
- **DEN — Davis Webb** (QB coach). Payton ceded play-calling. "Same offense, little tweaks." Waddle acquired by trade (2026 1st/3rd/4th).

### Offensive coordinators — cosmetic (HC calls plays)
PIT Angelichio ("an extension of Mike"; TE-development background), CLE Switzer (Ravens run-game coord), LV Janocko (Kubiak lieutenant, 4 stops), ARI Hackett (same as his GB role), BUF Carmichael (game-plans; booth vs sideline ⚠️), KC Bieniemy (collaborative), CHI Press Taylor, LAR Scheelhaase.

### Defensive coordinators — high leverage
- **BUF — Jim Leonhard** (Broncos asst HC/pass-game coord under Vance Joseph; ex-Wisconsin DC). 4-3 → 3-4, two-high shell, blitz way up. Added Bradley Chubb (3yr/$43.5M).
- **WAS — Daronte Jones** (Vikings DB coach under Flores). Disguise-heavy, blitz-heavy. Added Odafe Oweh (4yr/$100M), Chaisson.
- **SF — Raheem Morris** (fired Falcons HC; Rams DC SB LVI). Five-man fronts, "fever backer" hybrid; expected higher blitz rate (note: the 42.4% ATL blitz rate belongs to Ulbrich's unit, with Morris as HC). Eberflus asst HC. Odighizuwa added (from DAL).
- **GB — Jonathan Gannon** (fired Cardinals HC; Eagles DC). Says he's building "a new system" around the roster; prior stops two-high, sim pressures. Parsons on PUP (ACL, possible Wk 6); Rashan Gary → DAL, Quay Walker → LV.
- **DAL — Christian Parker** (Eagles DB coach under Fangio, 34). Two-high match zone; man-corner question ⚠️. Quinnen Williams (2025 deadline trade) + Kenny Clark inside; Rashan Gary added.
- **NYG — Dennard Wilson** (Titans DC). 75% zone, press everything, ~20% blitz. Burns/Carter/Thibodeaux/Reese; Dexter Lawrence traded to CIN.
- **PIT — Patrick Graham** (Raiders DC). 3-4 base, sim pressures over blitz; Ramsey planned at "star"/nickel, not CB1 (opened camp on PUP).
- **LV — Rob Leonard** (DL coach; Macdonald link via Ravens 2022). 4-3 → 3-4, heavy 3-3-5 sub. Nakobe Dean, Quay Walker added.
- **CLE — Mike Rutenberg** (Falcons pass-game coord). Schwartz resigned after being passed over for HC. Garrett → LAR; Jared Verse arrives.
- **HC-called defenses:** BAL Minter, MIA Hafley, TEN Saleh, NYJ Glenn (Sauce → IND, Quinnen → DAL were 2025 deadline trades).
